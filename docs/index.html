Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own API key? ▾

Yes. You bring your own DeepSeek or OpenAI API key. This keeps your data private and your costs under your control. DeepSeek is ~$0.14 per million tokens — very affordable.

How is the license key delivered? ▸

After purchase, you'll receive a license key by email within a few minutes. Enter it in the extension popup under the "Pro" tab along with your email address to activate unlimited access.

What payment methods are accepted? ▸

We accept credit/debit cards and major digital wallets. All payments are processed securely.

Can I cancel anytime? ▸

Yes. Pro is a monthly subscription. Cancel anytime and your license remains active until the end of the billing period. No long-term contracts.

Is my data secure? ▸

Absolutely. AI requests go directly from your browser to your configured AI provider (DeepSeek, OpenAI, etc.). We never see your messages, API keys, or chat data. Everything is stored locally in Chrome.

How does the 25/day free limit work? ▸

Free users get 25 AI operations per day (translations + suggestions + custom replies). The counter resets daily. Pro users have no limits.

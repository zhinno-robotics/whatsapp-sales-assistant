Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own API key? ▾

Yes. You bring your own DeepSeek or OpenAI API key. This keeps your data private and your costs under your control. DeepSeek is ~$0.14 per million tokens — very affordable for daily B2B use.

How is the license key delivered? ▸

After payment, you will receive your license key within a few minutes. Enter it in the extension popup under the "Pro" tab along with your email address to activate unlimited access.

What payment methods are accepted? ▸

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All payments are processed securely by our payment partner.

Can I cancel anytime? ▸

Yes. You can cancel your subscription anytime from the customer portal. Your access remains active until the end of the current billing period.

Is my data secure? ▸

Absolutely. AI requests go directly from your browser to your configured AI provider. We never see your messages, API keys, or chat data. Everything is stored locally in Chrome.

How does the 25/day free limit work? ▸

Free users get 25 AI operations per day (translations + suggestions + custom replies). The counter resets daily. Pro users have no limits.
